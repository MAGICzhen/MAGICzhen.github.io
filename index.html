About

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at the Picower Institute for Learning and Memory at MIT, work with Prof. Earl K. Miller

I received my PhD in Brain and Cognitive Sciences from the University of Rochester in 2023. My advisors were Prof. Krishnan Padmanabhan and Prof. Ralf Haefner.

My research spans computational neuroscience, systems neuroscience, dynamical systems, working memory, and cognition. I investigate how neural circuits support cognition and flexible behavior using mathematical analysis, computational modeling, and biologically realistic neural-network models.
